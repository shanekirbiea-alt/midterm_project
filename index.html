About Me

Hi! I'm Shane, a proud Bannatiran from the town called "Ili A Nadaeg" Banna Ilocos Norte. I am second year student from the University of Northern Philippines under the department of College of Communication and Information Technology studying the course Diploma in Computer Technology.

I'm an aspiring filmmaker. I spend most of my free time writing scripts, storyboarding my ideas, and shooting short films. For me, the true joy is in the process taking a story from a simple idea and bringing it to life, one frame at a time.
